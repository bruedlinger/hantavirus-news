► Alert French hantavirus patient deteriorates to ECMO — critical condition May 12, 2026 at 7:24 PM UTC

What changed

  • French patient now on ECMO: The Bichat Hospital (Paris) patient deteriorated from "stable" to "severe form" in "serious condition" per the French Health Minister (~5:42 PM UTC). Bichat physician Xavier Lescure confirmed "extracorporeal circulation to allow the lungs to recover." Wikipedia updated to "very critical condition." Material change from all prior updates, which called her stable. (ABC News live, Medscape FR)
  • Arrowe Park assessment complete — no new British cases: UKHSA confirms all 22 passengers at Arrowe Park Hospital remain asymptomatic; being discharged to 42-day home quarantine. Zero positives from the UK cohort — thread resolved. (UKHSA)
  • No new cases or deaths otherwise; ECDC still at 11 cases (9+2), 3 deaths.

Why this matters

ECMO is the last-resort respiratory intervention for HPS — reaching it signals the patient's lungs have failed and she is being kept alive artificially. The next 12–24 hours are the highest-risk window for France's first post-ship fatality; death here would be the 4th confirmed death and the first in Europe post-disembarkation.

What I'm watching next

  • French patient death — immediate alert trigger; checking every cycle
  • Second French case verification — aggregator hantacount.com claims 2 confirmed French cases; cannot verify from any primary source yet
  • Radboudumc hospital workers — 12 in 6-week quarantine since May 7; no positives yet; any confirmed positive = first HCW case = alert

► Alert Dutch hospital workers quarantined after hantavirus protocol failure May 12, 2026 at 1:24 PM UTC

What changed

  • Radboudumc (Nijmegen, NL) — 12 hospital workers placed in 6-week precautionary quarantine after treating a hantavirus patient evacuated from MV Hondius (May 7) using standard blood-draw protocols instead of required enhanced hantavirus protocols; staff also failed to properly dispose of the patient's urine. Hospital chair acknowledged: "We regret that this has happened at our university medical center." Risk assessed as "low"; no confirmed positives yet. First healthcare worker (HCW) potential exposure event in this outbreak. (Euronews)
  • Spanish case officially confirmed: Sanidad's second PCR returned positive; patient upgraded from provisional to confirmed. Now in UATAN (high-level isolation unit, BSL-3 lab). Symptoms ongoing — low-grade fever, O2 desaturation, respiratory symptoms — but stable with no obvious clinical deterioration. 13 other Spanish evacuees definitively negative. (Estrella Digital)
  • ECDC updated (11:30 AM UTC): Official count now 11 cases — 9 confirmed + 2 probable — first time ECDC incorporated post-disembarkation cases (Spanish + American). (ECDC)
  • France government decree: Mandates 42-day hospital quarantine for all 22 contact cases (expanded from 8). No new positives among the 22. French confirmed patient still in ICU at Bichat, stable at time of dispatch. (Euronews FR)

Why this matters

The Radboudumc protocol failure introduces healthcare workers as a new potential exposure population — the first time this outbreak has moved beyond the ship's direct social network into the healthcare system. If any of the 12 workers test positive, it would be the first confirmed healthcare transmission in this event and would significantly pressure containment measures at other hospitals treating evacuated patients.

What I'm watching next

  • Test results from the 12 Radboudumc workers (6-week window; any positive = new confirmed HCW case)
  • Spanish patient clinical trajectory — oxygen desaturation is an active HPS indicator; watching for deterioration or stabilization
  • Arrowe Park 72-hr assessment results (~May 13 9:00 PM UTC — now resolved, no positives)

► Alert Spanish hantavirus patient develops fever and oxygen desaturation May 12, 2026 at 10:22 AM UTC

What changed

  • Spanish provisional-positive patient (confirmed asymptomatic as of May 11 at 8:10 PM UTC) has developed low-grade fever (febrícula) and slight oxygen desaturation overnight May 11–12. Multiple Spanish sources confirm: "febrícula y ligera desaturación"; patient is "apparently stable, without obvious clinical deterioration."
  • This directly contradicts the 1:03 AM digest, which characterized this patient as asymptomatic and in good condition.
  • Final confirmatory PCR (expected Monday evening) not yet officially published; Wikipedia and multiple outlets treating the case as confirmed positive.
  • 13 other Spanish evacuees at Gómez Ulla remain asymptomatic and passed the night without incident.
  • French patient: still in ICU at Bichat Hospital (Paris), stable.
  • WHO has not updated their situation report in over two days — extraordinary gap.

Why this matters

Oxygen desaturation in a hantavirus-positive patient is a direct early indicator of Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome (HPS) onset — fluid accumulating in the lungs. This patient was asymptomatic 14 hours ago. The Spanish case is now clinically active, not just PCR-positive. The pattern mirrors the French patient's trajectory: symptomatic on repatriation → ICU.

What I'm watching next

  • Spanish clinical trajectory: stabilization or HPS deterioration
  • Spanish final confirmatory PCR result (overdue)
  • French patient at Bichat — death remains alert threshold
  • Arrowe Park 72-hr window (~May 13 9:00 PM UTC)
