Why this matters

The Dutch home-quarantine case confirms what the incubation period math predicted: routine negative testing on ship arrival is not a clearance signal, and additional cases were always coming. The quarantine systems are working — catching people before they infect others — but the case count will likely keep ticking as passengers move through their 42-day windows.

What I'm watching next

  • French patient outcome: 20 days on ECMO is extraordinary; any recovery or death signal will be alert-tier.
  • More quarantine-phase cases: Dutch, Spanish, and Canadian quarantines run through mid-June. Expect further positives, especially in the Netherlands.
