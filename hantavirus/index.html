First head-of-state level political statement on the outbreak. Health Minister Rist: all 22 contact cases now hospitalized; all positive cases exclusively cruise ship passengers. (Euronews May 13)
  • 26 Dutch passengers all initially negative (RIVM, May 12): All 26 Dutch repatriated passengers tested negative on initial screening; all in 6-week mandatory quarantine with weekly tests and daily GGD monitoring. (RIVM)
  • 10 St Helena/Ascension contacts transferred to UK HCID (UKHSA, May 12): 10 contacts from UK Overseas Territories being relocated to UK to complete isolation under NHS High Consequence Infectious Disease network supervision. None currently symptomatic. (UKHSA gov.uk)
  • Minnesota adds 1 monitoring case (MDH, May 12): Minnesota Dept of Health monitoring one person who had brief overseas exposure to a ship passenger who tested positive. No symptoms. Extends US monitoring beyond the ship cohort for the first time. (MDH statement)

Meanwhile the containment picture is holding: 26 Dutch passengers initially negative, all UK Arrowe Park nationals discharged to home quarantine with zero positives, and no deaths in 11 days.
