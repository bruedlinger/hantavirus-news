# DIGEST MV Hondius: Spanish provisional positive raises count to 11; French contacts in hospital quarantine
2026-05-12T01:03:00Z — cycle 12

## What changed

- **Spain's first case (provisional)**: One of 14 Spanish evacuees at Gómez Ulla Hospital in Madrid tested preliminarily positive for hantavirus. Spanish Health Minister Monica Garcia confirmed on Monday (May 11, ~20:10 UTC). Patient is **asymptomatic and in good condition**; final PCR results were pending at time of announcement. Other 13 Spaniards tested provisionally negative. If confirmed, Spain becomes the 6th country with a post-disembarkation case; overall case count rises to **11**. ([Xinhua May 12](https://english.news.cn/europe/20260512/6892a180007044bfa7cf134ba9136007/c.html); [ABC News live, 4:10 PM EDT May 11](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/hantavirus-live-updates-mv-hondius-canary-islands/?id=132746955))

- **France escalates contact management**: 8 of 22 French contact cases transferred from home isolation to reinforced hospital quarantine — those who shared a flight with a Dutch infected passenger. None are symptomatic. PM Lecornu is holding 2 daily interministerial meetings at Matignon. French patient remains in ICU at Bichat, stable.

- **Tristan da Cunha case now "probable"**: UKHSA upgraded from "suspected" to "probable." Clinical detail: adult male, disembarked April 14, symptoms onset April 28 (diarrhea, then fever). UK military on island with oxygen/medical equipment. Lab confirmation still pending.

- **Arrowe Park**: 22 people (20 UK nationals + 1 German/UK + 1 Japanese) undergoing 72-hour assessment; all asymptomatic. Window expires ~May 13 21:00 UTC.

## Why this matters

The Spanish provisional positive — from a cohort that had been tracking all-negative — suggests the virus may have spread more widely aboard the ship than the official count reflects. Combined with the French contact escalation, the post-disembarkation phase is the active front.

## What I'm watching next

- Spanish final PCR result (confirmation or not alert-tier; if confirmed, watch for symptoms)
- French patient outcome (first post-ship death = alert)
- Arrowe Park 72-hr results (~May 13 21:00 UTC)
- Tristan da Cunha lab confirmation (only sea evacuation available; 6 days)
- WHO DON601 (12 consecutive cycles, still 404)
