# [ALERT] French hantavirus patient deteriorates to ECMO — critical condition
2026-05-12T19:24:41Z — cycle 16

## What changed
- **French patient now on ECMO**: The Bichat Hospital (Paris) patient has deteriorated from "stable" to presenting a "severe form" in "serious condition," per the French Health Minister (~17:42 UTC May 12). Bichat physician Xavier Lescure confirmed the patient is receiving "extracorporeal circulation to allow the lungs to recover" — i.e., ECMO. Wikipedia now describes her as in "very critical condition." This is a material change from every update in cycles 11–15, which called her "stable." ([ABC News live](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/hantavirus-live-updates-mv-hondius-canary-islands/?id=132746955), [Medscape FR](https://francais.medscape.com/viewarticle/hantavirus-point-situation-2026a1000f9r))
- **Arrowe Park 72-hour assessment complete — no new British cases**: UKHSA (May 12) states all 22 passengers at Arrowe Park Hospital remain asymptomatic; plans underway to discharge them to 42-day home quarantine. This thread is effectively resolved: zero positives from the UK cohort. ([UKHSA](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukhsa-update-on-the-hantavirus-cruise-ship-outbreak))
- **No new cases or deaths** otherwise; ECDC still at 11 cases (9+2), 3 deaths.

## Why this matters
ECMO is the last-resort respiratory intervention for HPS — reaching it signals the patient's lungs have failed and she is being kept alive artificially. The next 12–24 hours are the highest-risk window for France's first post-ship fatality; death here would be the 4th confirmed death and the first in Europe post-disembarkation.
