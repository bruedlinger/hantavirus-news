# DIGEST May 18, 2026 daily summary

## What changed

- **MV Hondius docked Rotterdam** (morning May 18): Ship arrived Calandsteiger 7, Europoort. All 27 aboard (25 crew + 2 RIVM) tested by GGD on arrival; no symptoms reported; results awaited in quarantine. 3-day decontamination begins. All 26 passengers from 2nd/3rd repatriation flights confirmed negative. Philippine DMW: all 38 Filipino crew negative. ([Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/05/18/hantavirus-mv-hondius-cruise-ship-rotterdam-port/), RIVM May 18)
- **Ascension medic cleared** (UKHSA May 18): Samples taken to UK May 8 tested negative; no subsequent positive at Guy's/St Thomas' HCID; not a confirmed case. Thread closed. ([UKHSA gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukhsa-update-on-the-hantavirus-cruise-ship-outbreak))
- **UK acquires favipiravir from Japan** (UKHSA May 18): UK received doses of favipiravir (experimental antiviral) from Japan as preparedness stockpile. Drug is investigational only — no proven human efficacy against Andes virus; evidence limited to in vitro and animal studies. ([SMC expert reaction](https://www.sciencemediacentre.org/expert-reaction-to-latest-ukhsa-hantavirus-update-including-receiving-from-japan-doses-of-the-antiviral-medicine-favipiravir-favi/))
- **ECDC May 18**: 12 total (9+2+1), 3 deaths. New language: "additional cases anticipated given incubation period." Risk EU/EEA: very low. ([ECDC](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/infectious-disease-topics/hantavirus-infection/surveillance-and-updates/andes-hantavirus-outbreak))
- **French patient (Bichat ECMO)**: Still alive, ~264+ hours on ECMO. No death or recovery signal. Critically ill.
- **At least 7 Arrowe Park contacts** cleared for home 45-day isolation by May 16 following negative tests (UKHSA).
- **Genomic paper published** (PMC12201636): Virological characterization of outbreak ANDV — near-identical genomes across secondary cases; single amino acid change post-isolation; non-adapted wild-type; confirms single introduction. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41012669/))
