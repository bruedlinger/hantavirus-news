# ALERT US Confirmed Hantavirus Case Resolves Negative: Dr. Kornfeld Exits Biocontainment
2026-05-14T09:44:48Z — cycle 25

## What changed
- **Dr. Stephen Kornfeld, 69, Oregon oncologist** — the HHS-announced "confirmed US case" from May 11 — received **subsequent NEGATIVE PCR results** at Nebraska Medicine and was moved from the Biocontainment Unit to the National Quarantine Unit alongside 15 other passengers. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/13/health/hantavirus-doctor-biocontainment-nebraska), [ABC News live 9:37 PM EDT May 13](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/hantavirus-live-updates-mv-hondius-canary-islands/?id=132746955)
- **CDC confirmed the case is "inconclusive"**: initial positive came from testing abroad (Tenerife/transit); Nebraska Medicine retesting shows negative. Results expected "within a day or so" of May 13 evening. [WOWT](https://www.wowt.com/2026/05/13/cdc-says-previously-confirmed-hantavirus-case-brought-nebraska-medicine-now-inconclusive/)
- **Three-agency discrepancy now resolves**: WHO (DON601: "inconclusive"), CDC (situation page: "no US cases"), HHS (May 11: "confirmed") effectively align — HHS's announcement was based on a weak preliminary positive not holding up under retesting.
- Kornfeld helped care for ill passengers aboard MV Hondius and developed "flu-like illness" — he was an onboard responder, not merely an asymptomatic contact.

## Why this matters
The "first confirmed US hantavirus case" narrative (widely reported May 11–14) is being walked back. If retesting confirms negative, the US case count returns to 0 and official WHO total drops from 11 to 10. Kornfeld's identification also confirms the confirmed-positive individual's identity for the first time.
