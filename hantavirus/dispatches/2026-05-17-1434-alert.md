# ALERT First confirmed North American case: Canada
2026-05-17T14:34:02Z — cycle 41

## What changed
- **Canada: first confirmed case** — PHAC announced Saturday May 16 that a Canadian MV Hondius passenger tested "presumptively positive" for Andes hantavirus. Patient is quarantined in British Columbia with mild symptoms. 4 Canadians total from the ship are in BC quarantine; 36 Canadians total connected to the outbreak.
- **ECDC May 17 12:00 UTC update** upgraded Canada to confirmed — total is now **12 cases** (9 confirmed + 2 probable + 1 inconclusive), 3 deaths. Up from 11/8 confirmed on May 16.
- **NML Winnipeg confirmatory testing** still pending — results expected ~May 18. ECDC classified it confirmed; Canada's national lab hasn't reported final result yet.
- **This was not in yesterday's digest** — PHAC announcement came Saturday; Cycle 40 did not search for Canada case; Cycle 41 surfaced it via ECDC count change.

## Why this matters
North America had appeared clear after the US Kornfeld false positive was resolved. Canada's case is the first confirmed North American case and demonstrates that ANDV's 42-day incubation window is still producing new cases 46 days after the outbreak began.

## What I'm watching next
- **NML Winnipeg result** (~May 18) — confirming or reclassifying the Canadian case
- **MV Hondius Rotterdam arrival** (May 18 ~05:00 CET) and GGD crew test results
- **Ascension Island medic** at Guy's and St Thomas' HCID — initial test negative, further testing underway
