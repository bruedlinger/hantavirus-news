## What happened on May 17

- **Canada confirms first North American case**: PHAC presumptive positive (announced May 16) upgraded to confirmed by ECDC May 17 12:00 UTC update; NML Winnipeg lab confirmation received May 17 (earlier than anticipated). Patient is a Yukon resident in their 70s, treated at Victoria hospital (BC) with mild symptoms (fever, headache onset ~May 15). Travelling companion (also Yukon, 70s) confirmed **NEGATIVE** by NML — first within-couple negative confirmation in the outbreak.
