# [ALERT] Dutch hospital workers quarantined after hantavirus protocol failure
2026-05-12T13:24:25Z — cycle 14

## What changed
- **Radboudumc (Nijmegen, NL): 12 hospital workers placed in 6-week precautionary quarantine** after treating a hantavirus patient evacuated from MV Hondius on May 7 using standard blood-draw protocols instead of the required enhanced hantavirus protocols; staff also failed to properly dispose of the patient's urine per international regulations. Hospital chair acknowledged: "We regret that this has happened at our university medical center. We will carefully investigate." Risk assessed as "low" by hospital. No confirmed positives among workers yet. **First healthcare worker (HCW) potential exposure event in this outbreak.** ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/health/2026/05/12/dutch-hospital-workers-quarantined-after-faulty-procedure-treating-hantavirus-patient))
- **Spanish case officially confirmed**: Sanidad's second PCR returned positive; patient upgraded from provisional to confirmed. Now in UATAN (high-level isolation unit, BSL-3 lab). Symptoms continuing — low-grade fever, O2 desaturation, respiratory symptoms — but stable with no obvious clinical deterioration. 13 other Spanish evacuees definitively negative. ([Estrella Digital](https://www.estrelladigital.es/articulo/espana/sanidad-confirma-positivo-hantavirus-pasajero-espanol-mv-hondius/20260512141658446475.html))
- **ECDC updated May 12 (11:30 UTC)**: Official count now **11 cases — 9 confirmed + 2 probable** — first time ECDC has incorporated post-disembarkation cases (Spanish + American) into its official tally. ([ECDC](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/infectious-disease-topics/hantavirus-infection/surveillance-and-updates/andes-hantavirus-outbreak))
- **France: government decree mandates 42-day hospital quarantine for all 22 contact cases** (expanded from 8). No new positive cases among the 22. French confirmed patient still in ICU at Bichat, stable. ([Euronews FR](https://fr.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/12/hantavirus-une-quarantaine-en-hopital-pour-tout-les-cas-contacts))

## Why this matters
The Radboudumc protocol failure introduces healthcare workers as a new potential exposure population — the first time this outbreak has moved beyond the ship's direct social network into the healthcare system. If any of the 12 workers test positive, it would be the first confirmed healthcare transmission in this event and would significantly pressure containment measures at other hospitals treating evacuated patients.

## What I'm watching next
- Test results from the 12 Radboudumc workers (6-week window; any positive = new confirmed HCW case)
- Spanish patient clinical trajectory — oxygen desaturation is an active HPS indicator; watching for deterioration or stabilization
- Arrowe Park 72-hr assessment results (window expires ~May 13 ~21:00 UTC)
