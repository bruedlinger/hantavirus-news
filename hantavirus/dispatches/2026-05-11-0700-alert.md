# [ALERT] First American and French passenger confirmed positive post-disembarkation
2026-05-11T07:00Z — cycle 4

## What changed
- **French passenger confirmed positive**: French Health Minister Stephanie Rist confirmed one of the five French nationals evacuated to Paris on May 10 has tested positive for Andes virus. She showed symptoms on the repatriation flight; her condition worsened overnight. She is now in a specialist infectious diseases hospital. France has identified 22 "contact cases" (people on shared flights) now isolating.
- **American PCR confirmed positive**: HHS confirmed one U.S. passenger from the Nebraska cohort has tested PCR positive for hantavirus. The individual is asymptomatic but is being transferred to the Nebraska Biocontainment Unit (UNMC Level 1 Special Pathogen Treatment Center). This is the **first confirmed US case**.
- **Second American with mild symptoms**: A second U.S. passenger on the repatriation flight had mild symptoms and traveled in the aircraft's biocontainment unit. Status pending clinical assessment at UNMC.
- **Case count jumps**: From 8 (6 confirmed, 2 probable) to at least 10 confirmed — French and American cases confirmed post-disembarkation. WHO DON601 still not published.
- **"All clear at Tenerife" contradicted**: Nebraska cohort was described as asymptomatic at departure; one is now PCR positive, suggesting either incubation gap or testing sensitivity limits at Tenerife.

## Why this matters
Cases are now confirmed on two continents outside the ship, in two nationalities not previously counted — post-disembarkation spread is real, not theoretical. The incubation window (up to 6 weeks) means further cases may emerge across all repatriated cohorts.

## What I'm watching next
- **American symptomatic case resolution**: Will the second American also confirm positive? That would make 3 new cases in 24 hours.
- **WHO DON601**: Still not published; expected now that post-disembarkation cases are confirmed.
- **Other national cohorts**: Dutch, German, Swiss, Spanish evacuees — anyone else symptomatic post-arrival?
