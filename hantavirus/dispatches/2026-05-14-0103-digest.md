# DIGEST WHO DON601 published after 23-day delay; French patient survives second day on ECMO; Spanish patient improving
2026-05-14T01:03:08Z — cycle 24

## What changed
- **WHO DON601 finally published (May 13, data as of 17:00)**: After 23 consecutive cycles returning 404, WHO's third Disease Outbreak News report is live. It formally counts 11 cases (8 confirmed + 2 probable + 1 inconclusive), 3 deaths, CFR 27%. Notably, WHO classifies the US case as **"inconclusive" with mixed lab results undergoing retesting** — WHO has not formally confirmed the US case despite HHS's May 11 announcement. Near-identical genetic sequences across cases confirm a single-spillover event. ([WHO DON601](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON601))
- **French patient (Bichat, ECMO) — survived second day**: Bichat physician Xavier Lescure (~13:30 UTC May 13) described "vital prognosis compromised, next hours will be determining" — roughly 12 hours elapsed with no death reported by cycle end (22:02 UTC May 13). hantacase.com: 0 deaths in France. No hantavirus deaths since May 2, now 12 days. Patient remains critically ill on ECMO. ([hantacase.com](https://hantacase.com/country/france))
- **Spanish patient (UATAN) improving**: Health Minister Mónica García (~14:04 UTC May 13) confirmed patient "improved somewhat" after oxygenotherapy. First clearly positive trajectory signal since admission. 13 other Spanish evacuees asymptomatic. ([Infobae live blog](https://www.infobae.com/))
- **Italy thread resolved negative**: All 4 quarantined persons in Italy (British tourist + companion at Milan/Sacco; man from Calabria; South African at Padua; Argentinian woman at Messina) tested negative for hantavirus. Italian health ministry issued 42-day quarantine circular. ([The Local Italy](https://www.thelocal.it/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/))

WHO DON601's long-delayed publication resolves the most persistent open thread in this monitoring effort — and its characterization of the US case as "inconclusive" rather than confirmed creates a new divergence between WHO and HHS that warrants watching. The French patient's survival past Lescure's "next hours" window is the best clinical signal of the day, though ECMO prognosis remains precarious. The containment picture held all day: no new cases, no new deaths, Italy's four quarantines all clear.
