Previous Day — May 19, 2026

► Digest Cases surge to 536, second DRC province confirmed, Berlin hospital named, WHO vaccine advisory convenes May 19, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC

What changed

  • Case count climbed from 336 to 536 suspected (plus 105 probable / 34 confirmed / 134 deaths) — ~60% jump over 36 hours. DRC Health Ministry morning: 513/131; evening update: 536/105/34/134.
  • Butembo (North Kivu) confirmed as new case location — spread from Ituri to a second major DRC province; Butembo was the epicenter of the 2018–2020 Zaire Ebola outbreak. Nine health zones in Ituri now affected.
  • Fort Portal (western Uganda) burial-linked cluster — Ugandans who attended a DRC burial returned to western Uganda developing symptoms; taken to Fort Portal for treatment. Lab confirmation still pending.
  • Charité university hospital (Berlin) confirmed as the facility treating Dr. Peter Stafford + 6 contacts; Stafford identified as missionary with Serge Christian mission organization. German health ministry confirmed special isolation ward.
  • WHO R&D Blueprint advisory group convened to deliberate whether to deploy Ervebo (Zaire-approved vaccine) off-label against Bundibugyo. Gavi confirmed 2,000 doses available in DRC. No final recommendation announced.
  • Genomic preprint (virological.org): INRB + CPHL sequenced 3 near-complete BDBV genomes confirming a new spillover event, phylogenetically distinct from 2007 and 2012 outbreaks. Third documented Bundibugyo outbreak.
  • DRC humanitarian response 34% funded; WFP airlift delivered 5+ tonnes of supplies to Ituri; DRC opening 3 new Ebola treatment centers.
  • WHO chief Tedros expressed "serious concern about scale and speed" of the epidemic.

Why this matters

May 19 marked the outbreak's fastest acceleration yet — geographic spread to a second DRC province, a cross-border burial cluster in Uganda, and near-doubling of the case count signal that containment is not holding. The WHO vaccine advisory deliberating off-label Ervebo use is the response community's most consequential decision point since the PHEIC declaration.
