What changed

  • Uganda HCW cluster confirmed; alert issued. Cases 6 and 7 are both healthcare workers at an unnamed private Kampala hospital, exposed through patients linked to Case 1. Uganda total: 7 confirmed / 1 death. Three of seven Uganda cases (43%) are HCWs — nosocomial amplification signal in the capital. Uganda suspended public transport to DRC.
  • Case count growing fast. DRC hit 112 confirmed (Wikipedia, May 25–26), up from 101 on May 24 (+11%). Suspected held at ~906. ECDC updated to 121 confirmed / 1,077 suspected by end of day.
  • $500M pledged at May 25 ministerial meeting. Nearly doubled from $208M on May 23. Key donors: OCHA ($60M), US State Dept ($23M), Gates Foundation ($15M), South Africa ($5M). Disbursement timelines unknown.
  • Obeldesivir named as PEP candidate. WHO DG May 25 briefing: Gilead GS-5245 (oral remdesivir prodrug) for post-exposure prophylaxis alongside two mAbs (MBP134 + Regeneron). Trial "being developed" with Africa CDC — not yet initiated as of May 26.
  • DON604 still absent. 5+ days overdue since DON603 (May 21). No new geographic spread confirmed.

Why this matters

Uganda's HCW rate (43%) signals healthcare settings are becoming amplifying nodes — the same dynamic that accelerated the 2018–2020 DRC Zaire outbreak. Nosocomial chains in Kampala's private hospital sector would make containment significantly harder.
