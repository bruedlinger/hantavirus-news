Why this matters

Confirmed DRC cases nearly tripled since the May 17 WHO DON (12 → 51), signaling either genuine transmission acceleration or significantly expanded testing capacity. The WHO EC’s retroactive PHEIC validation closes a procedural anomaly; the pending formal recommendations may include vaccination strategy, ring-vaccination protocols, and border guidance that could materially affect national policy.
