Confirmed cases nearly tripled since the May 17 WHO DON (12→51 DRC), signaling either genuine transmission acceleration or improved testing. The WHO EC's retroactive PHEIC validation closes a procedural anomaly; watch for formal temporary recommendations that may include travel guidance or ring-vaccination protocols.
