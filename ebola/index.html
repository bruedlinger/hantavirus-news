New findings
  • WHO / PHEIC PHEIC declared May 17 without convening Emergency Committee — unprecedented in WHO history. Bundibugyo ebolavirus, no approved vaccine or treatment.
  • DRC / Ituri 336 suspected / 89 deaths / 12 confirmed as of May 17. Three health zones: Bunia, Rwampara, Mongbwalu. Patient zero: nurse, Bunia, symptoms onset April 24.
  • Uganda / Kampala 2 confirmed cases, both imported from DRC. Case 1 (59-yr Congolese male) died May 14. Case 2 confirmed May 16. No local transmission confirmed.
  • DRC / Goma 1 confirmed case in Goma (wife of Bunia victim, traveled while infected). Goma is partially under M23 Movement control — response access unclear.
  • DRC / Kinshasa Ruled out. Initial report of Kinshasa case disproven — confirmatory tests were negative (WHO confirmed).
  • Healthcare workers 4 HCW deaths within 4 days at Mongbwalu General Referral Hospital. First suspected case was a nurse. Significant IPC breach.
  • Detection gap 3-week gap between first suspected death (April 24) and official outbreak confirmation (May 15). WHO: "low clinical index of suspicion among HCPs."
  • Pharma / response Gilead, Merck, J&J, Regeneron, Roche, Abbott at Africa CDC coordination meeting May 15. Clinical trials for Bundibugyo candidates recommended by WHO.
