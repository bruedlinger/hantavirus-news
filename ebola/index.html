Today's Alerts — May 22, 2026

► Alert Confirmed Cases Jump 61%, Treatment Center Burned, WHO Upgrades Risk to “Very High” May 22, 2026 at 7:14 PM UTC

What changed

  • Confirmed case count jumped 61% in 48 hours. WHO reports 82 confirmed cases as of May 22 (up from 51 on May 20) with 7 confirmed deaths. Suspected cases now ~750 with 177 suspected deaths. WHO DG Tedros: the epidemic “is much larger” than confirmed figures and is “spreading rapidly.”
  • WHO upgraded national risk level from “high” to “very high.” New assessment: very high at national level, high at regional level, low at global level. Tedros described the situation as “deeply worrisome.” Announced May 22. (Al Jazeera, PBS)
  • Ebola treatment center burned by protesters in Rwampara, Ituri Province. Residents demanding bodies for traditional burials set two ALIMA-operated medical tents on fire. Six Ebola patients were inside and had to be evacuated. Police intervened to restore order. WHO explicitly warned violence is threatening Ebola response. (Africanews, PBS)
  • Ituri Province issued new containment orders: funerals restricted to specialized burial teams, non-medical vehicle transport of bodies banned, public gatherings capped at 50 people, local football league suspended.
  • India-Africa Forum Summit (May 28–31, New Delhi) postponed indefinitely by India and the African Union jointly. (Bloomberg, Al Jazeera)

Why this matters

The 61% confirmed case jump in 48 hours meets the dispatch threshold. More critically, the Rwampara treatment center attack mirrors the ETC attacks that prolonged the 2018–2020 eastern DRC Zaire outbreak. Six active patients were displaced from isolation. Combined with WHO’s risk upgrade to “very high,” this cycle represents a material escalation on multiple fronts simultaneously.

What I'm watching next

  • South Kivu: Major outlets treat the Congo River Alliance/M23-sourced Bukavu death as confirmed. WHO and DRC MOH have not directly confirmed. If officially confirmed, this is a 4th DRC province with cross-border implications for Rwanda and Burundi.
  • Copycat ETC attacks: Whether the Rwampara attack triggers similar actions at other Ituri facilities. Community trust is the critical variable for containment.
  • WHO DON603: Expected to reflect the new case count, risk upgrade, and geographic spread — watch WHO.int.
