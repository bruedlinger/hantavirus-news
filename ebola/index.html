New findings
  • Scale record Largest Bundibugyo outbreak ever. Previous record: 131 cases (Uganda, 2007). At 336 suspected, this is historically unprecedented for this strain. (Imperial College)
  • Detection gap Imperial College: pre-detection death volume suggests outbreak circulated weeks or months before April 24 — true spillover likely earlier than the official index case.
  • US / Americans Six Americans being evacuated to US or US military base in Germany. At least one may have symptoms; testing pending. (STAT News, May 17)
  • Security / ADF ADF (Allied Democratic Forces) confirmed active in Ituri — separate armed threat from M23 in Goma. Two distinct armed groups hampering access and contact tracing.
  • USAID gap USAID dismantling has structurally weakened contact tracing, border screening, and lab capacity — deficit vs. prior DRC Ebola responses. (Gounder, May 2026)
  • MSF capacity MSF has 2 transit centres in Ituri (Bwanasura + Bunia); 3 suspected cases isolated at Salama clinic, Bunia. Large-scale response mobilizing.
  • Treatment RCTs 4 experimental candidates for RCT protocols: monoclonal antibodies, Gilead remdesivir IV, oral remdesivir analog, +1. Oxford/Moderna broad-spectrum vaccine early-stage (CEPI/EU, $26.7M).
