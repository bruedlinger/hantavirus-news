Today's Alerts — May 19, 2026

► Alert PHECS declared, cases surge 52% to 513, Butembo confirmed, burial cluster reaches western Uganda May 19, 2026 at 11:30 AM UTC

What changed

  • Africa CDC formally declared PHECS (May 18, after last alert) — First-ever Public Health Emergency of Continental Security for an Ebola outbreak. Emergency Consultative Group chaired by Prof. Salim Abdool Karim. $2M mobilized; joint IMST with WHO activated. (Africa CDC)
  • Case count officially 513 suspected / 131 deaths — DRC Health Minister Samuel Roger Kamba statement, May 19. Up from 336/89 on May 17: ~52% more suspected cases, ~47% more deaths in two days. Kamba cautioned deaths are “suspected, investigations underway.” (ABC News)
  • Butembo (North Kivu) now confirmed as case location — Geographic spread to a second major DRC province beyond Ituri; Butembo was epicenter of the 2018–2020 Zaire outbreak. Multiple sources confirm (ABC News, PBS, CIDRAP). WHO primary source verification still pending.
  • Fort Portal, Uganda: burial-linked suspected cases — Ugandans who attended a burial in eastern DRC returned to western Uganda (~250 km from Kampala); some developed Ebola symptoms and were taken to Fort Portal for treatment. Cross-border travel while symptomatic = potential uncontrolled transmission chain. Lab confirmation pending.

Why this matters

The outbreak is simultaneously deepening (more than 50% case and death surge in ~48 hours) and widening geographically (Butembo in North Kivu, Fort Portal in western Uganda). The detection mechanism failure — wrong lab cartridges used for three weeks — suggests historic case counts have been systematically understated. The PHECS signals Africa CDC believes this is not under control at continental scale.

What I'm watching next

  • Fort Portal and Butembo lab confirmation — especially Fort Portal (new Uganda geography, burial-chain transmission)
  • WHO Emergency Committee convening date and temporary recommendations
  • Stafford colleagues’ test results from Germany; German hospital case registration (RKI)
  • Next WHO DON publication for updated confirmed case count

Previous Day — May 18, 2026

► Digest PHEIC declared, American physician infected, first-ever US Ebola travel ban, Africa CDC declares PHECS May 18, 2026 — daily digest

What changed

  • WHO declared PHEIC — May 17 declaration without Emergency Committee (first time in WHO history); does not meet “pandemic emergency” criteria but is a formal international emergency.
  • Dr. Peter Stafford confirmed Ebola-positive — American physician at Nyankunde Hospital, Bunia; first US national confirmed with Bundibugyo. Transported to Germany with 6 additional US contacts for monitoring. Hospital undisclosed.
  • US imposes first-ever Ebola travel ban (Title 42) — Foreign nationals from DRC, Uganda, South Sudan barred from US entry for 30 days. South Sudan included preventively (no confirmed cases). US and Rwanda both openly defying WHO “no travel restrictions” guidance.
  • Africa CDC formally declared PHECS — First-ever PHECS for an Ebola outbreak; ECG chaired by Prof. Salim Abdool Karim; $2M mobilized; IMST activated with WHO under “4 Ones” model.
  • Case count escalated significantly — 246/80 (May 15) → 336/89 (May 17) → ~395/106 at PHECS declaration. Three-week detection delay explained by incorrect lab cartridges (Zaire, not Bundibugyo).
  • Rwanda closed all DRC border crossings — Primary source: Rubavu District Mayor Mulindwa; effective May 17, indefinitely; daily water-fetching by DRC residents prohibited.
  • PPE stockpile warning — Africa CDC: 6–8 week replenishment window if transmission escalates. Mongbwalu mining zone identified as probable outbreak origin.

Why this matters

May 18 was the most consequential day of the outbreak: a named American healthcare worker infected, two independent emergency declarations (PHEIC + PHECS), and the first US Ebola travel ban ever. The detection delay being explained by wrong lab cartridges suggests the surveillance gap was partly systemic, not just access-related.

  • WHO Emergency Committee convening date and temporary recommendations
  • Stafford colleagues’ test results; German hospital registration
