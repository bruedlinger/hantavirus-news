Previous Day — May 28, 2026

► Digest Uganda closes border; WHO names treatment candidates; Tedros calls for ceasefire; Mongbwalu confirmed escape May 28, 2026 at 1:11 AM UTC

What changed

  • Uganda closed its DRC border for 4 weeks (May 27). All DRC returnees face mandatory 21-day self-isolation. Mass gatherings banned in Kampala and border districts. Third country explicitly defying WHO's no-travel-restriction guidance (after Rwanda, US). (Al Jazeera, Euronews)
  • WHO expert advisory (May 28) settles the treatment question. Confirmed cases: MBP134 + Maftivimab® + Remdesivir (combination). PEP contacts: obeldesivir. Ervebo explicitly ruled out outside trials. Vaccine pipeline: Oxford/Serum ChAdOx1 Bundibugyo 2–3 months to trial readiness; IAVI rVSV 7–9 months. Maftivimab already on the ground in DRC. (WHO.int)
  • Tedros visited DRC (May 28) and called for ceasefire. "We cannot build community trust or isolate the sick while bombs are falling." "A catastrophic collision of disease and conflict." >90% of cases in Ituri. (Al Jazeera)
  • US +$80M bilateral assistance (May 27) — brings US total significantly above $23M pledged May 25. (Later confirmed: $112M bilateral + $50M + $300M OCHA = $462M total.)
  • CDC upgraded Uganda travel advisory to Level 2 (May 27).
  • Mongbwalu ETC attack (May 23) documented — missed in prior cycles. 18 suspected escaped; 1 of 4 tested = POSITIVE. Confirmed Ebola case was circulating in community. Dr. Lokudi confirmed. (PBS)
  • Case counts (cycle 19 baseline): DRC 121 confirmed / 1,077 suspected / 17 confirmed deaths. Uganda: 7 confirmed / 1 death. (ECDC May 27. Updated: ECDC May 29 shows DRC 125/906, Uganda 9.)

Why this matters

Three countries defying WHO guidance signals international coordination breakdown. The Mongbwalu escape is qualitatively the worst containment failure yet — a confirmed positive was directly released into the community. WHO's treatment advisory is the first concrete roadmap; Maftivimab is already on the ground.
