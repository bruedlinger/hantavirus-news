Today's Alerts — June 6, 2026

► Alert Ebola Confirmed in ADF/IS-Controlled Mambasa — Zero Response Access; CDC Projects 20,000+ Cases June 6, 2026 at 6:15 PM UTC

What changed

  • Mambasa, Ituri Province confirmed Ebola case location — Mambasa is run by Allied Democratic Forces (ADF), an Islamic State-affiliated militia. Health workers cannot travel there; area is “chaotic and violent,” making contact tracing and isolation impossible. (CIDRAP)
  • South Kivu burial team attack — Residents attacked an Ebola burial team in South Kivu province, exposing the coffin and body of a confirmed Ebola patient. Community resistance has now reached the third confirmed province. (CIDRAP)
  • DRC cases and deaths surged June 1–5 — DRC: 381–452 confirmed / 64–82 confirmed deaths (ECDC vs. CDC as of June 5), up from 282/42 on June 1. Ituri now has 17 health zones confirmed (up from 7 in DON605, May 29). (ECDC June 5; CDC June 5)
  • Uganda doubled to 19 confirmed / 2 deaths — up from 9/1 on June 1. Three new cases June 5, all contacts of prior confirmed cases. All Uganda cases traced to DRC importation per CDC; no independent community transmission in Kampala confirmed. (CDC Newsroom June 5)
  • CDC worst-case projection released June 5 — If only 20% of cases enter isolation within 2 days of symptom onset (current trajectory), more than 20,000 cases are projected in 2 of 3 model scenarios over the next 3 months. At 70% isolation rate: 94% probability of staying under 10,000. Some scenarios could exceed the 2014–2016 West Africa outbreak (28,000+ cases). (CDC Newsroom June 5)

Why this matters

Mambasa is the first confirmed outbreak location in this epidemic that is structurally inaccessible to the response apparatus. Unlike the ETC attacks in Rwampara and Mongbwalu — one-time incidents that could be addressed with security forces — Mambasa cannot be entered at all while ADF controls it. The CDC projection quantifies the containment stakes: current isolation rates may be heading toward a catastrophic scenario exceeding the largest Ebola outbreak in history.

What I'm watching next

  • Mambasa case count: Can DRC MOH or WHO quantify the Mambasa cluster separately? Any humanitarian negotiation with ADF for access?
  • South Kivu burial attack: Was the exposed body recovered? How many people were exposed at the attack scene?
  • Treatment trials (PARTNERS + EBO-PEP BUNDI): Expected to begin “shortly” per EMA June 3. Watch ClinicalTrials.gov and ANRS for formal registration.
  • DON606: No new WHO situation report since May 31. Overdue — watch who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news.
