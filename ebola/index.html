New findings
  • DRC / Mongbwalu MISSED (May 23): Residents burned MSF tent at Mongbwalu hospital; 18 suspected patients escaped into community. Of 4 tested: 1 POSITIVE — a confirmed Ebola case circulated freely in the community. Hospital director Dr. Richard Lokudi confirmed. Second ETC attack in 2 days (after Rwampara May 21).
  • Uganda border Closed for 4 weeks (May 27). Only response/humanitarian/cargo/security allowed to cross. Mandatory 21-day self-isolation for all DRC returnees. Mass gatherings banned in Kampala and border districts. Third country defying WHO no-travel-restrictions guidance (after Rwanda, US).
  • WHO advisory First countermeasure guidance (May 28): Three therapeutics for confirmed cases: MBP134 + Maftivimab® (Regeneron) + Remdesivir; combination therapy recommended. Obeldesivir for PEP contacts. Ervebo explicitly excluded from use outside clinical trials. Maftivimab supply already on ground in DRC.
  • Vaccine pipeline Oxford/Serum Institute ChAdOx1 Bundibugyo: 2–3 months from trial readiness (needs animal data). IAVI rVSV Bundibugyo: 7–9 months. All products must be tested in clinical trials only.
  • Tedros / DRC WHO DG and emergencies chief Ihekweazu visited DRC (May 28). Called for "immediate ceasefire." "We cannot build community trust or isolate the sick while bombs are falling." >90% of cases are in Ituri Province.
  • US funding State Dept finalized additional $80M bilateral assistance (May 27). Brings US total significantly above $23M pledged May 25.
  • CDC / Uganda Uganda travel advisory upgraded Level 1 → Level 2 (May 27). DRC remains Level 3.
  • Case counts No May 28–29 update yet. Latest (ECDC, May 27): DRC 121 confirmed / 1,077 suspected / 17 confirmed deaths / 238 suspected deaths. Uganda 7/1.
