Today's Alerts — May 26, 2026

► Alert Uganda HCW Cluster Grows: Two More Healthcare Workers Infected at Private Kampala Hospital May 26, 2026 at 7:30 PM UTC

What changed

  • Uganda confirmed cases rise from 5 to 7 (as of May 25): Cases 6 and 7 are both healthcare workers at an unidentified private hospital in Kampala, exposed through caring for patients connected to the Congolese man who died May 11. (Uganda MOH, PBS)
  • 3 of Uganda’s 7 confirmed cases (43%) are now healthcare workers — Cases 4, 6, and 7. This pattern signals active nosocomial amplification in Uganda’s private hospital sector.
  • A second HCW cluster at a Kampala private facility suggests infection control protocols failed at multiple sites, not just the index hospital where Case 1 died.
  • The identity of the private Kampala hospital has not been disclosed; full contact tracing scope (staff, patients, visitors) is unknown.

Why this matters

Three healthcare worker infections in Uganda’s capital — 43% of all Uganda cases — is a warning sign that Kampala’s private hospital sector lacks adequate IPC for Ebola patients. If contacts of Cases 6–7 are not rapidly identified and isolated, this cluster can amplify independently in Uganda, separate from the DRC outbreak.

What I'm watching next

  • Name and location of the private Kampala hospital; contact counts from that facility
  • Whether Cases 6–7 generate secondary infections among hospital contacts
  • Uganda MOH IPC sweep of all healthcare facilities that treated any Case 1 chain patient
  • Fort Portal burial cluster — still not lab-confirmed; separate potential transmission chain

Previous Day — May 25, 2026

► Digest Ebola outbreak crosses 1,000 total cases; confirmed count up 23% in 72 hours May 25, 2026

What changed

  • DRC confirmed cases reached 101 (CDC, May 24), up 23% from 82 on May 22 — the fastest confirmed-case growth rate since the outbreak began; Wikipedia aggregated total crossed 1,000 cases for the first time (1,010 total; 231 deaths)
  • Uganda remained stable at 5 confirmed cases / 1 death; no new Uganda cases reported on May 24–25 (Cases 6–7 announced later that day)
  • 79th World Health Assembly concluded (May 19–23) without issuing an Ebola-specific emergency resolution or new funding pledge; WHO DG cited the outbreak in closing remarks as evidence of ongoing global vulnerability
  • South Sudan joined the Kampala cross-border coordination meeting alongside WHO, DRC, and Uganda — first active South Sudan engagement in the formal response structure
  • Kinshasa definitively ruled out: WHO confirmatory test (May 17) was negative; prior Wikipedia listing was erroneous
  • CDC travel advisory for DRC confirmed at Level 3 (Reconsider Nonessential Travel); Uganda at Level 1 — resolves a long-running discrepancy with CIDRAP’s Level 4 claim
  • WHO DON604 not published as of May 25; DON603 (May 21) is overdue for a follow-up given the outbreak’s pace

Why this matters

Crossing the 1,000-case threshold marks an order-of-magnitude escalation beyond both prior Bundibugyo outbreaks combined (2007: 131 cases; 2012: 38 cases). The absence of a WHO situation report update is a transparency gap as confirmed cases accelerate and contact tracing remains critically stalled at 21%.

What I'm watching next

  • WHO DON604 — overdue; expected to update confirmed counts, health zone spread, and contact tracing rate
  • Ervebo off-label deployment decision — DRC and Uganda government approvals still pending; Gavi 2,000 doses in-country
  • Fort Portal (Uganda) lab results — burial-linked suspected cases still unconfirmed
