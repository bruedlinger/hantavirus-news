Previous Day — May 23, 2026

► Digest Uganda HCW infection confirmed; suspected cases near 1,000; contact tracing at 21%; GeneXpert blind to this strain May 23, 2026

What changed

  • Uganda reaches 5 confirmed cases — first healthcare worker infection. Three new cases confirmed May 23: a driver, a healthcare worker, and a Congolese woman, all linked to Case 1. The HCW case signals local nosocomial transmission has begun in Uganda.
  • Suspected cases approaching 1,000. Wikipedia's aggregated count reached 968 suspected / 96 confirmed / 216 deaths as of May 23 — up from WHO DG's 750/82/177 on May 22.
  • Contact tracing catastrophically low: 21%. WHO DON603 reports 1,603 contacts identified in Ituri alone; only ~337 actively followed. ~1,270 known contacts untracked.
  • GeneXpert cannot detect Bundibugyo. WHO EC formal temporary recommendations (May 22) explicitly warned all countries to use RT-PCR only. DRC's most widely deployed point-of-care diagnostic is blind to this strain.
  • DON603 confirms 15 health zones and South Kivu. 15 health zones affected across Ituri, Nord-Kivu, and South Kivu — up from 11. South Kivu is officially the 4th DRC province.
  • Tanzania screening at Lake Tanganyika. Active border screening and simulation drills; traders cross from DRC within 12 hours.
  • Rwampara ETC conditions. Xinhua (May 22): ~50 suspected cases / 2–4 daily deaths at time of attack; patients "fled into surrounding neighborhoods."

Why this matters

Uganda HCW infection is a qualitative shift — the virus is establishing transmission chains inside healthcare facilities in the second affected country. Combined with a 21% contact follow-up rate and a diagnostic platform that can't detect the strain, the outbreak is structurally outpacing the response.
