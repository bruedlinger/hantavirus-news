# Ebola Story State — 2026 Ituri Province (Bundibugyo) Outbreak

*Last updated: 2026-05-18T00:05:00Z — Cycle 1 (baseline)*

---

## Summary

A Bundibugyo ebolavirus (BDBV) outbreak began in DRC's Ituri Province in late April 2026, with initial spread to Uganda's capital Kampala and a confirmed case in Goma (North Kivu, under M23 control). WHO declared a PHEIC on May 17, 2026 — notably without convening an Emergency Committee, which is unprecedented. There are no approved vaccines or treatments for the Bundibugyo strain (existing licensed vaccines target Zaire). As of May 17, 336 suspected cases and 89 deaths, with 12 lab-confirmed cases. This is DRC's 17th Ebola outbreak since 1976.

---

## Confirmed Facts (with sources + dates)

### Core Epidemiology

- **Virus:** Bundibugyo ebolavirus (BDBV) / *Orthoebolavirus bundibugyoense* — confirmed via PCR and genomic sequencing by INRB (Institut National de Recherche Biomédicale)
- **CFR:** Historical 30–50% for Bundibugyo (2007 and 2012 outbreaks)
- **Incubation period:** 2–21 days
- **Reservoir:** Fruit bats suspected (zoonotic origin)
- **No approved vaccines or treatments** for Bundibugyo strain; two WHO-prequalified vaccines (rVSV-ZEBOV, Ad26.ZEBOV/MVA-BN-Filo) target only Zaire strain

### Case Counts (as of May 17, 2026 — WHO/Wikipedia)

| Category | Count |
|---|---|
| Suspected cases | 336 |
| Lab-confirmed cases | 12 |
| Deaths (suspected) | 89 |
| Healthcare workers dead | 4 (Mongbwalu General Referral Hospital, within 4 days) |

Earlier reported figures (May 15–16, per WHO DON, Africa CDC): 246 suspected / 80 deaths / 8–13 confirmed — numbers updated rapidly as surveillance scaled up.

### Geography

**DRC — Primary:**
- Ituri Province: Bunia, Rwampara, Mongbwalu health zones (8 confirmed cases as of May 15)
- Goma, North Kivu: 1 confirmed case (wife of Bunia victim traveled to Goma while infected; Goma is partially under M23 Movement control — response access unclear)

**Uganda — Cross-border:**
- Kampala: 2 confirmed cases (both imported from DRC)
  - First: 59-year-old Congolese male, admitted Kibuli Muslim Hospital May 11, died May 14
  - Second: confirmed May 16, no local transmission identified at time of report

**DRC — Ruled out:**
- Kinshasa: initial case report DISPROVEN — confirmatory tests were negative (WHO confirmed)

### Patient Zero / Timeline

- **April 24, 2026:** First suspected case (nurse, Bunia) developed symptoms; died within 3 days
- **May 5, 2026:** WHO alerted to potential outbreak; experts deployed to Ituri
- **May 14, 2026:** First positive lab results from INRB
- **May 15, 2026:** DRC health authorities officially confirmed outbreak; Africa CDC convened urgent regional coordination meeting with DRC, Uganda, South Sudan + 30+ partners
- **May 16, 2026:** 2nd Uganda case confirmed; WHO DG indicated PHEIC declaration imminent
- **May 17, 2026:** WHO declared PHEIC — Tedros declared without convening an Emergency Committee (**first time ever**)

### PHEIC Declaration — Notable Details

- Declared May 17, 2026 by WHO DG Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus
- **Unprecedented:** First PHEIC declared without Emergency Committee recommendation — International Panel for Pandemic Preparedness called this a reflection of "the gravity of the situation"
- WHO explicitly recommends: **No country should close its borders or restrict travel/trade**
- Does NOT meet criteria for "pandemic emergency"

### Demographics / Transmission Pattern

- Majority affected: age 20–39 years
- >60% female — suggests "significant risks associated with household and caregiver transmission"
- Amplified in healthcare settings (inadequate IPC protocols)
- Enhanced by unsafe burial practices
- 3-week gap between first suspected death (April 24) and official confirmation (May 15) — WHO noted "low clinical index of suspicion among healthcare providers"

### Contact Tracing (as of May 15)

- 65 contacts listed; 15 identified as high-risk
- Follow-up hampered by insecurity and movement restrictions
- Several listed contacts died before isolation (tracing gap)

### Security Context

- Ituri Province has been "engulfed in a yearslong conflict" (STAT News)
- Goma case is in area under M23 Movement control — response access uncertain
- Mining-related population movement in Rwampara and Bunia
- Refugee displacement from regional conflicts

### Response Actions

- WHO rapid response teams deployed to Ituri since May 5
- Africa CDC: Urgent coordination meeting May 15 with 30+ partners including WHO, UNICEF, FAO, US CDC, ECDC, China CDC, pharma companies (Gilead, Merck, J&J, Regeneron, Roche, Abbott), Gates Foundation, Wellcome Trust
- MSF mobilizing teams and resources
- US gov arranging quarantined transport for exposed American citizens
- US Embassy health alerts: Kampala (May 15), Kinshasa (May 15), Juba/South Sudan (May 17)
- CDC Travel Advisories: DRC = Level 2 (Ituri Province preexisting Level 4); Uganda = Level 1
- Exit screening at airports and border crossings for febrile illness
- Clinical trials for candidate therapeutics/vaccines recommended by WHO IHR

### Background: DRC Ebola History

- This is DRC's **17th Ebola outbreak** since virus identification in 1976
- Most recent prior outbreak: declared over December 1, 2025 (64 cases, 45 deaths — different strain, Zaire)
