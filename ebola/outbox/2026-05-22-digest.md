# DIGEST Ebola May 22: WHO upgrades DRC risk to "very high," ETC attack, South Kivu spreads
2026-05-22T23:59:00Z — cycle 13

## What changed
- **WHO upgraded DRC national risk from "high" to "very high"** (regional: high; global: low). Tedros: "spreading rapidly," "deeply worrisome," outbreak "much larger" than confirmed figures.
- **Case count: 82 confirmed / ~750 suspected / 177 suspected deaths** — 61% jump in confirmed cases in 48 hours (51 on May 20 → 82 on May 22).
- **Rwampara ETC attack (May 21):** Protesters burned two ALIMA tents at Rwampara Hospital demanding body access; 6 Ebola patients evacuated. ALIMA field coordinator confirmed calm restored and operations resumed; new ETC tent being erected in hospital courtyard.
- **Ituri Province emergency orders (May 22):** Funerals restricted to specialized teams; body transport banned; public gatherings capped at 50; local football league suspended.
- **South Kivu:** Multiple major outlets (Al Jazeera, France 24, Bloomberg) reported confirmed Ebola death near Bukavu, citing Congo River Alliance/M23 spokesman. WHO DON603 (published May 21) subsequently officially confirmed geographic expansion into South Kivu.
- **India-Africa Forum Summit (May 28–31, New Delhi) postponed indefinitely** — announced jointly by India MEA and African Union.
- **Kenya:** 34,500+ travelers screened; 7 tested and cleared; isolation points at Lokiriama (Kenya-Uganda border) and Nadapal (Kenya-South Sudan border) established.

## Why this matters
The WHO risk upgrade combined with the Rwampara ETC attack and South Kivu spread signal a multi-front deterioration: accelerating confirmed case counts, community resistance threatening the response infrastructure, and geographic expansion beyond the original Ituri/North Kivu zone. The "very high" national risk designation is WHO's near-maximum escalation short of global.

- Uganda case expansion (burial-linked Fort Portal cluster; additional Kampala contacts)
- WHO DON603 contact tracing figures and South Kivu assessment details
