# DIGEST May 20, 2026 — Ebola Bundibugyo Outbreak
2026-05-21T01:29:00Z — cycle 8

## What happened May 20

- **Case count updated (WHO DG briefing, May 20):** ~600 suspected cases / 51 DRC confirmed / 2 Uganda confirmed / 1 Germany confirmed (Stafford) / 139 suspected deaths. Confirmed cases up significantly from 35 (Wikipedia morning) to 51 (WHO briefing evening). ([WHO DG briefing](https://www.who.int/news-room/speeches/item/who-director-general-s-opening-remarks-at-the-media-briefing-on-ebola-outbreak-in-drc-and-uganda-20-may-2026))
- **WHO Emergency Committee convened May 19** — EC retroactively validated the PHEIC declaration; agreed the situation constitutes a PHEIC. DG noted "serious concern about scale and speed." Formal EC temporary recommendations pending publication. ([WHO EC remarks](https://www.who.int/news-room/speeches/item/who-director-general-s-opening-remarks-at-the-emergency-committee-on-ebola-epidemic-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda---19-may-2026))
- **WHO added $3.4M emergency funds** → total WHO response budget now $3.9M. (WHO DG briefing, May 20)
- **Uganda postponed Martyrs' Day** — annual pilgrimage drawing ~2 million people postponed as mass-gathering prevention. (WHO DG briefing, May 20)
- **100,000+ newly displaced** due to the outbreak itself, compounding access challenges. (WHO EC opening remarks, May 19)
- **Stafford stable at Charité Berlin** — "feels good," eating, receiving monoclonal antibodies. Wife Dr. Rebekah Stafford (38) and colleague Dr. Peter LaRochelle (46) under monitoring; no positive tests. ([ABC News](https://abcnews.go.com/International/ebola-outbreak-suspected-death-toll-rises-139-warns/story?id=133142454))
- **USAID funding collapse documented** — HHS DRC: $33M (FY2024) → $10M (FY2025); USAID DRC: $1.2B → $67M (Q4 FY2025). IRC reduced Ituri operations from 5 to 2 areas. Former officials confirm surveillance was gutted before the outbreak. ([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/19/us-aid-cuts-hamper-drc-ebola-response/))
- **Ervebo advisory: still no decision** — WHO R&D Blueprint met May 19; no DRC/Uganda government decision on off-label Bundibugyo use. 2,000 doses in DRC, ~45,000 additional potentially available.
- **Fort Portal (Uganda) and Butembo (North Kivu):** Both remain in play — Fort Portal burial-linked suspected cases not yet lab-confirmed; Butembo confirmed but pending WHO primary source.

## Why this matters
Confirmed cases nearly tripled since the May 17 WHO DON (12→51 DRC), signaling either genuine transmission acceleration or improved testing. The WHO EC's retroactive PHEIC validation closes a procedural anomaly; watch for formal temporary recommendations that may include travel guidance, vaccination strategy, or ring-vaccination protocols.

## What I'm watching next
- WHO EC formal temporary recommendations — publication on WHO.int
- Fort Portal lab confirmation (Uganda)
- Ervebo deployment decision from DRC/Uganda governments
- WHO DON603
