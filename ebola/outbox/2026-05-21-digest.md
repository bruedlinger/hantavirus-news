# DIGEST May 21, 2026 — Bundibugyo Ebola Outbreak (DRC/Uganda)
2026-05-22T03:20:00Z — cycle 11

## What happened May 21

- **South Kivu case claimed, unverified:** M23 militia spokesman announced a 28-year-old from Kisangani died of Ebola in Bukavu, South Kivu (~500km south of Ituri epicenter). DRC government has not confirmed; WHO silent. Travel history is anomalous — Kisangani (Tshopo province) has no documented outbreak cases. If independently confirmed, would be spread to a fourth DRC province at the Rwanda/Burundi/DRC border triangle. Treat as unverified until official lab confirmation.

- **Vaccine pipeline: critical clarification:** INRB Director Muyembe told Al Jazeera DRC is "awaiting experimental Oxford vaccine shipments from US and UK." However, the Oxford/CEPI/Moderna multivalent filovirus vaccine program (funded $26.7M in January 2026) is **preclinical only** — no human trials, no shipments possible. Muyembe's claim is unresolved; what DRC is actually expecting (Ervebo off-label? BARDA mAbs compassionate use?) remains open.

- **Czechia contact isolated:** 1 American (not confirmed positive) transported to Bulovka Hospital, Prague at US request; strict isolation up to 21 days. Czech Health Minister Vojtěch confirmed. Total evacuees: Stafford + 5 contacts at Charité Berlin; 1 contact at Bulovka Prague.

- **Case count:** ~575 suspected / 51 DRC confirmed / 148 deaths (DRC/Uganda MOH, May 21). 11 health zones across Ituri + Nord-Kivu. WHO airlift: 18 tonnes of medical supplies to Ituri. 800+ contacts tracked in DRC (up from 65 on May 15).

- **Goma airport confirmed closed** since M23 seized the city in January 2025 — significant barrier to air-based humanitarian response in North Kivu.

- **South Africa pledged $2.5M** to Africa CDC Africa Epidemics Fund for surveillance, lab capacity, IPC, and cross-border preparedness.
