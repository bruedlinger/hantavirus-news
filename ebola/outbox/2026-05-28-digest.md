# DIGEST May 27–28: Uganda closes border; WHO names treatment candidates; Tedros calls for ceasefire
2026-05-29T01:11:00Z — Cycle 19

Note: No monitoring cycle ran on May 28 UTC (missed day). This digest covers confirmed developments from May 27–28.

## What changed

- **Uganda closed its DRC border for 4 weeks (May 27).** Only authorized response teams, humanitarian operations, food/cargo transport, and essential security personnel may cross — all subject to strict health screening. Anyone returning from DRC must undergo mandatory 21-day self-isolation under health official supervision. Uganda also banned mass gatherings in Kampala and DRC border districts. Uganda is now the third country (after Rwanda and the US) explicitly defying WHO's "no travel restrictions" guidance. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/5/28/uganda-closes-border-with-drc-in-an-effort-to-contain-ebola-outbreak), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/05/28/uganda-shuts-border-with-dr-congo-as-cases-of-rare-ebola-type-surge))

- **WHO expert advisory (May 28) resolves the treatment/vaccine question.** Three therapeutics prioritized for confirmed cases: MBP134 + **Maftivimab® (Regeneron)** + Remdesivir; combination therapy recommended. Obeldesivir for post-exposure prophylaxis of contacts. **Ervebo explicitly ruled out** — WHO experts stated it "should not be used outside carefully designed research settings." Vaccine pipeline named for the first time: Oxford/Serum Institute ChAdOx1 Bundibugyo potentially 2–3 months from clinical trial readiness (needs additional animal data); IAVI rVSV Bundibugyo is 7–9 months out. **Maftivimab supply is already on the ground in DRC.** All products to be used exclusively within clinical trials. ([WHO.int](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-05-2026-experts-convened-by-who-advise-on-candidate-treatments-and-vaccines-for-ebola-disease-caused-by-bundibugyo-virus))

- **Tedros visited DRC (May 28) and called for a ceasefire.** WHO DG and WHO emergencies chief Dr. Chikwe Ihekweazu flew to DRC. Tedros issued a public message: "We cannot build community trust or isolate the sick while bombs are falling." Described the situation as "a catastrophic collision of disease and conflict." More than 90% of all cases remain in Ituri Province. ([WHO.int](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-05-2026-message-by-the-who-director-general-to-the-people-of-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/27/drc-facing-catastrophic-collision-of-ebola-and-war-who-chief-warns))

- **US State Dept announced additional $80M bilateral assistance (May 27).** Brings total US Ebola-specific commitment significantly above the $23M pledged on May 25. Partner breakdown unavailable. ([State.gov](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/05/ebola-response-update-may-28-2026))

- **CDC upgraded Uganda travel advisory from Level 1 to Level 2 (May 27).** DRC remains at Level 3.

- **Missed development corrected (May 23): Second ETC attack in Mongbwalu.** Residents attacked and burned an MSF tent at Mongbwalu General Referral Hospital on Friday night, May 23 — two days after the Rwampara ETC attack. 18 suspected Ebola patients escaped into the community. Lab testing of 4 patients returned 1 positive result: **a confirmed Ebola case was circulating freely in the community** at the time of the attack. Hospital director Dr. Richard Lokudi confirmed the escape. Whether that confirmed case was subsequently traced and isolated is unknown. ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/eighteen-suspected-ebola-patients-escape-after-treatment-tent-is-set-on-fire-for-a-second-time-in-congo), CNN, BNO News)

- **Case counts unchanged.** Latest: DRC 121 confirmed / 1,077 suspected / 17 confirmed deaths / 238 suspected deaths (ECDC, May 27). Uganda: 7 confirmed / 1 death. No May 28 DRC MOH update seen.

## Why this matters

Three countries are now explicitly defying WHO's no-travel-restriction guidance, and the Mongbwalu escape means at least one confirmed case has been directly released into the community through an attack — a qualitatively worse outcome than the Rwampara fire, where patients were "evacuated." The WHO treatment advisory is the first concrete roadmap for countermeasures; with Maftivimab already on the ground, trial initiation could be imminent.

## What I'm watching next

- Whether the Mongbwalu confirmed escaped case has been traced — watch DRC MOH, WHO DON604
- Formal trial registration for obeldesivir and Maftivimab — watch ClinicalTrials.gov, Africa CDC
- Outcome of Tedros's DRC visit: any ceasefire response, new response commitments, or health zone access agreements
- DON604: still overdue (now 8+ days since DON603); "DON 2026-E000253" seen on WHO page — may be new numbering
