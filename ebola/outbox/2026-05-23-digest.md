# DIGEST May 23, 2026 — Uganda HCW infection, 1,000 suspected threshold approaching, contact tracing at 21%

2026-05-24T01:55:00Z — cycle 14

## What happened

- **Uganda reaches 5 confirmed cases — first healthcare worker infection.** Three new cases confirmed May 23 (Bloomberg): a driver, a healthcare worker, and a Congolese woman, all linked to Case 1 (the Congolese man who died in Kampala May 14). The HCW case is the first confirmed healthcare worker infection in Uganda and signals local nosocomial transmission has begun.
- **Suspected cases approaching 1,000.** Wikipedia's aggregated count (DRC MOH + regional sources) reached 968 suspected / 96 confirmed / 216 deaths as of May 23 — up from WHO DG's 750/82/177 on May 22. The discrepancy reflects DRC MOH figures exceeding WHO's confirmed count; both indicate rapid growth.
- **Contact tracing catastrophically low: 21%.** WHO DON603 (published May 21, confirmed May 23) reports 1,603 contacts identified in Ituri province alone; only ~337 actively followed. ~1,270 known contacts are untracked. This is the primary containment failure driving continued transmission.
- **GeneXpert — the standard DRC point-of-care test — cannot detect Bundibugyo.** WHO EC formal temporary recommendations (published May 22) explicitly warned all countries to use RT-PCR, not GeneXpert. This means DRC's most widely deployed diagnostic tool is blind to this outbreak.
- **DON603 confirms 15 health zones and South Kivu.** 15 health zones now affected across Ituri, Nord-Kivu, and South Kivu — up from 11. South Kivu is officially the 4th DRC province.
- **Tanzania screening at Lake Tanganyika.** Active border screening and simulation drills at crossings with DRC; traders cross within 12 hours.
- **On-the-ground: Rwampara ETC conditions before attack.** Xinhua (May 22) reports the facility had ~50 suspected cases and 2–4 daily deaths at the time protesters burned it; patients "fled into surrounding neighborhoods" — more chaotic than the official "6 evacuated" narrative.

## Why this matters

Uganda HCW infection is a qualitative shift — it confirms the virus is establishing transmission chains inside healthcare facilities in the second affected country. Combined with a 21% contact follow-up rate and a diagnostic platform that can't detect the strain, the outbreak is structurally outpacing the response.
