# DIGEST May 24, 2026 — Ebola Bundibugyo Outbreak Crosses 1,000 Cases; WHA Concludes With Pandemic Warning
2026-05-25T12:10:49Z — cycle 15

## What changed (May 24 UTC)

- **Cases cross 1,000:** As of May 24, DRC reports 101 confirmed / 904 suspected cases — confirmed up 23% from 82 on May 22. Wikipedia aggregated total: 1,010 suspected+confirmed / 231 deaths / 106 confirmed. Uganda remains at 5 confirmed / 1 death (no new Uganda cases on May 24). Sources: CDC current situation, Wikipedia.
- **79th World Health Assembly concluded (May 19-23):** WHA held in Geneva throughout the week of the outbreak's escalation. WHO DG Tedros briefed member states on Ebola twice (opening remarks May 19, separate Ebola+hantavirus info session May 22). WHA adopted pandemic preparedness resolutions and health architecture reform agenda. No Ebola-specific emergency resolution or new funding pledge issued. WHA closing remarks (May 23) cited Ebola as evidence of ongoing global vulnerability. Source: WHO news-room, UN News.
- **South Sudan joins Kampala coordination:** Cross-border coordination meeting held in Kampala with WHO, South Sudan, DRC, and Uganda — first active South Sudan engagement in coordinated response. Previously South Sudan was only monitored as preventive inclusion in US travel ban. Source: UN News.
- **CDC travel advisory confirmed Level 3:** CDC current situation page confirms DRC at Level 3 (Reconsider Nonessential Travel), Uganda at Level 1 — resolves the ongoing contradiction with CIDRAP's Level 4 claim. Level 3 is authoritative. Airport screening confirmed active at Washington-Dulles and Atlanta. Source: CDC.
- **Kinshasa definitively not affected:** WHO May 17 confirmatory testing of the single reported Kinshasa case was negative. Wikipedia listing Kinshasa as affected is erroneous. Kinshasa remains officially unaffected — not a new development, but now confirmed via multiple sources. Source: WHO, multiple search confirmations.
- **No WHO DON604 published:** DON603 (May 21) remains the most recent official situation report. Next report now overdue. Contact tracing rate (21%), GeneXpert inadequacy, and geographic spread to 15 health zones remain last official figures.
- **Concurrent hantavirus cluster:** WHO referenced a multi-country Andes hantavirus cluster linked to travel from Argentina at WHA May 22 session — separate outbreak, no connection to Ebola. Flagged for awareness.

## Why this matters

Confirmed cases are growing steadily (23% in ~48 hours) and the outbreak has crossed the symbolic 1,000-case threshold when suspected and confirmed are combined. The WHA concluded without specific Ebola commitments, meaning the response funding gap (34% of $1.4B humanitarian appeal funded) remains unaddressed at the highest level of global health governance.
