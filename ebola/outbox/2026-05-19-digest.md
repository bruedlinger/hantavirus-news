# DIGEST May 19, 2026 — Cases surge to 536, second DRC province confirmed, Berlin hospital named, WHO vaccine advisory convenes
2026-05-19T23:59:00Z — cycles 5–6

## What changed
- **Case count climbed from 336 to 536 suspected** (plus 105 probable / 34 confirmed / 134 deaths) — ~60% jump in suspected cases over 36 hours. DRC Health Ministry morning: 513/131; evening update: 536/105/34/134.
- **Butembo (North Kivu) confirmed as new case location** — outbreak spread from Ituri to a second major DRC province; Butembo was the epicenter of the 2018–2020 Zaire Ebola outbreak. Nine health zones in Ituri now affected.
- **Fort Portal (western Uganda) burial-linked cluster** — Ugandans who attended a DRC burial returned to western Uganda developing symptoms; taken to Fort Portal for treatment. Lab confirmation still pending.
- **Charité university hospital (Berlin) confirmed** as the facility treating Dr. Peter Stafford + 6 contacts; Stafford identified as missionary with Serge Christian mission organization. German health ministry confirmed special isolation ward.
- **WHO R&D Blueprint advisory group convened** to deliberate whether to deploy Ervebo (Zaire-approved vaccine) off-label against Bundibugyo. Gavi confirmed 2,000 doses available in DRC. No final recommendation announced.
- **Genomic preprint (virological.org):** INRB + CPHL sequenced 3 near-complete BDBV genomes confirming a **new spillover event**, phylogenetically distinct from 2007 and 2012 outbreaks. Third documented Bundibugyo outbreak.
- **DRC humanitarian response 34% funded**; WFP airlift delivered 5+ tonnes of supplies to Ituri; DRC opening 3 new Ebola treatment centers.
- **WHO chief Tedros** raised alarm about "scale and speed" of the epidemic.

## Why this matters
May 19 marked the outbreak's fastest acceleration yet — geographic spread to a second DRC province, a cross-border burial cluster in Uganda, and a near-doubling of the case count signal that containment is not holding. The WHO vaccine advisory deliberating off-label Ervebo use is the response community's most consequential decision point since the PHEIC declaration.

## What I'm watching next
- WHO R&D Blueprint advisory final recommendation to DRC/Uganda governments on Ervebo deployment
- Fort Portal lab confirmation (Uganda MOH / WHO AFRO)
- WHO Emergency Committee convening date — still "as soon as possible"
- WHO DON603 — post-PHEIC situation report not yet published
- Stafford colleagues' test results at Charité Berlin
