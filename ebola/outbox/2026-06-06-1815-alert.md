# ALERT Ebola Confirmed in ADF/Islamic State-Controlled Mambasa — Zero Response Access; CDC Projects 20,000+ Cases
2026-06-06T18:15:00Z — cycle 23

## What changed

- **Mambasa, Ituri Province now confirmed Ebola case location** — Mambasa is run by Allied Democratic Forces (ADF), an Islamic State-affiliated militia. Health workers cannot travel there. The area is "chaotic and violent," making contact tracing and isolation impossible. ([CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/new-world-screwworm/ebola-spreads-area-under-islamic-state-control-cases-mount))
- **South Kivu burial team attack** — Residents attacked an Ebola burial team in South Kivu province, exposing the coffin and body of a confirmed Ebola patient. Community resistance has now reached the third confirmed province. ([CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/new-world-screwworm/ebola-spreads-area-under-islamic-state-control-cases-mount))
- **DRC cases and deaths surged** — DRC: 381–452 confirmed / 64–82 confirmed deaths as of June 5 (range reflects ECDC vs. CDC methodology; both are current). That is up from 282 confirmed / 42 deaths on June 1 — a +35–60% rise in confirmed cases and +52–95% rise in deaths in 5 days. Ituri now has **17 health zones** confirmed (up from 7 in DON605, May 29). ([ECDC June 5](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-virus-disease-outbreak-democratic-republic-congo-and-uganda); [CDC June 5](https://www.cdc.gov/ebola/situation-summary/index.html))
- **Uganda doubled to 19 confirmed / 2 deaths** — up from 9 confirmed / 1 death on June 1. All Uganda cases remain linked to DRC importation per CDC; no independent community transmission in Kampala confirmed. ([CDC June 5](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/update-on-ebola-outbreak-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda-6-5-2026.html))
- **CDC worst-case projection released June 5** — If only 20% of cases enter isolation within 2 days of symptom onset (as currently occurring), more than 20,000 cases are projected in 2 of 3 model scenarios over the next 3 months. At 70% isolation rate: 94% probability of staying under 10,000. Some scenarios could exceed the 2014–2016 West Africa outbreak (28,000+ cases). ([CDC Newsroom June 5](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/update-on-ebola-outbreak-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda-6-5-2026.html))

## Why this matters

Mambasa is the first confirmed outbreak location in this epidemic that is structurally inaccessible to the response apparatus. Unlike the ETC attacks in Rwampara and Mongbwalu — incidents that could be addressed with security — Mambasa cannot be entered at all while ADF controls it. Combined with the CDC projection showing the current isolation trajectory leads to catastrophic scale, this represents a qualitative shift in outbreak risk.

## What I'm watching next

- **Mambasa case count:** How many confirmed cases in ADF-controlled territory? Can DRC MOH or WHO quantify the Mambasa cluster separately?
- **ADF/IS engagement:** Any humanitarian negotiation or ceasefire affecting ADF-held areas? Tedros's ceasefire appeal targeted unnamed groups — any response from ADF?
- **South Kivu burial team attack consequences:** Was the exposed body recovered? How many contacts were present at the attack?
