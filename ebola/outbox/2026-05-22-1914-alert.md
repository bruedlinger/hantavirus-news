# ALERT — Confirmed Cases Jump 61%, Treatment Center Burned, WHO Upgrades Risk to "Very High"
2026-05-22T19:14Z — cycle 12

## What changed

- **Confirmed case count jumped 61% in 48 hours.** WHO reports 82 confirmed cases as of May 22 (up from 51 on May 20) with 7 confirmed deaths. Suspected cases are now ~750 with 177 suspected deaths (up from ~600/139 on May 20). WHO DG Tedros: the epidemic "is much larger" than confirmed figures and is "spreading rapidly."
- **WHO upgraded national risk level from "high" to "very high."** New assessment: very high at national level, high at regional level, low at global level. Tedros described situation as "deeply worrisome." Announced May 22. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/22/who-raises-ebola-public-health-risk-to-very-high-in-dr-congo), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/who-chief-says-ebola-outbreak-in-congo-is-spreading-rapidly-and-upgrades-risk-assessment))
- **Ebola treatment center burned by protesters in Rwampara, Ituri Province.** Residents demanding bodies for traditional burials set two ALIMA-operated medical tents on fire. Six Ebola patients were inside and had to be evacuated. Police intervened to restore order. WHO explicitly warned violence is threatening Ebola response. ([Africanews](https://www.africanews.com/2026/05/21/drc-ebola-hospital-set-on-fire-as-protesters-demand-access-to-bodies-of-dead-relatives/), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/residents-burn-ebola-treatment-center-in-congo-as-anger-grows-over-the-outbreak))
- **Ituri Province issued new containment orders** in direct response: funerals restricted to specialized burial teams, non-medical vehicle transport of bodies banned, public gatherings capped at 50 people, local football league suspended.
- **India-Africa Forum Summit (May 28–31, New Delhi) postponed indefinitely** by India and the African Union jointly, citing "the evolving health situation in parts of Africa." ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/india-africa-summit-postponed-indefinitely-over-ebola-outbreak), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/21/india-africa-summit-postponed-as-ebola-spreads-to-m23-held-dr-congo-area))

## Why this matters

The 61% confirmed case jump in 48 hours meets the dispatch threshold. More critically, the Rwampara treatment center attack is the kind of community resistance that prolonged the 2018–2020 eastern DRC Zaire outbreak — ETCs were attacked repeatedly in that outbreak, each time complicating containment and destroying trust. Six active patients were displaced from isolation. Combined with WHO's risk upgrade to "very high," this cycle represents a material escalation on multiple fronts simultaneously.

## What I'm watching next

- **South Kivu:** Multiple major outlets (Al Jazeera, France 24, Bloomberg) are reporting a confirmed Ebola death in Bukavu — but WHO and DRC MOH have not issued a direct official confirmation; source remains a Congo River Alliance (M23-affiliated) spokesperson. If officially confirmed, this is a 4th DRC province with serious cross-border implications for Rwanda and Burundi.
- **Copycat ETC attacks:** Whether the Rwampara attack triggers similar actions at other facilities in Ituri. Community trust is the critical variable for containment.
- **WHO DON603:** Expected to reflect the new case count, risk upgrade, and geographic spread — watch WHO.int.
