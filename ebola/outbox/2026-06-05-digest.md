# DIGEST Ebola Outbreak — June 2–5, 2026
2026-06-06T18:15:00Z — cycle 23 (consolidated catch-up; no cycles ran June 2–5)

## What changed

- **WHO drastically revised suspected case count (June 3):** Suspected cases dropped from ~906 to 116 after laboratory testing cleared most patients. This is not an improvement signal — medical experts (IMC) say "the situation remained serious." Confirmed count rose to 321/48 (DRC) in the same WHO revision, up from 282/42 on June 1. ([CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/ebola/who-drastically-downsizes-ebola-case-count-dr-congo-outbreak))
- **DRC cases surged June 2–5:** From 321 confirmed (June 3 WHO revision) to 381–452 confirmed / 64–82 deaths (ECDC/CDC June 5). Ituri now has 17 affected health zones, up from 7 in DON605. ([ECDC June 5](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-virus-disease-outbreak-democratic-republic-congo-and-uganda))
- **Uganda cases doubled:** From 9 (June 1) to 19 confirmed / 2 deaths (June 5). Three new cases reported June 5 alone, all contacts of confirmed cases. All Uganda cases traced to DRC importation — no independent Kampala transmission chain confirmed. ([CDC June 5](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/update-on-ebola-outbreak-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda-6-5-2026.html))
- **Ebola confirmed in ADF/IS-controlled Mambasa:** Health workers cannot access the area; contact tracing is impossible. ([CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/new-world-screwworm/ebola-spreads-area-under-islamic-state-control-cases-mount))
- **South Kivu burial team attacked:** Residents exposed coffin and body of confirmed Ebola patient — third province with active community resistance to response. ([CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/new-world-screwworm/ebola-spreads-area-under-islamic-state-control-cases-mount))
- **CDC worst-case projection (June 5):** 20% isolation rate → >20,000 cases in 3 months in 2/3 model scenarios. 70% isolation rate → 94% probability of <10,000. ([CDC Newsroom](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/update-on-ebola-outbreak-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo-and-uganda-6-5-2026.html))
- **Africa CDC + WHO launched joint continental Ebola response plan (June 5).** ([WHO emergency page](https://www.who.int/emergencies/situations/ebola-outbreak---drc-2026))
- **Kenya quarantine facility legal dispute:** High Court (Judge Patricia Nyaundi) temporarily blocked US-Kenya facility for up to 50 Americans exposed in DRC. Two protesters killed. Government pushing ahead despite court order. Court ordered disclosure within 7 days; next hearing June 23. ([CNBC Africa](https://www.cnbcafrica.com/2026/two-killed-in-kenya-protest-against-us-ebola-quarantine-facility-protest-organiser-says); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/2/high-court-orders-kenya-government-to-release-details-of-ebola-facility))
- **Treatment trials progressing but not yet initiated:** EMA Emergency Task Force met June 3 to discuss trial designs. PARTNERS (treatment) and EBO-PEP BUNDI (PEP) trials expected to begin "shortly." No ClinicalTrials.gov registration found yet. ([Pharma Tech](https://www.pharmtech.com/view/ema-collaborates-with-african-regulators-on-ebola-treatments))
- **No new countries affected.** No treatment approved. No ceasefire from ADF or M23.

## Why this matters

The 5-day gap saw the outbreak grow substantially while spreading into territory structurally inaccessible to the public health response. The CDC has now publicly quantified the worst case: outbreak scale comparable to 2014–2016 West Africa if isolation rates don't improve.

## What I'm watching next

- **Mambasa cluster size and any ADF engagement**
- **Treatment trial formal initiation (ClinicalTrials.gov, ANRS, ISARIC)**
- **Kenya quarantine facility hearing June 23**
- **DON606** — no new WHO DON published since May 31 joint statement; overdue
