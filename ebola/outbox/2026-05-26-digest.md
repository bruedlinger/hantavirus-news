# DIGEST May 26, 2026 — Bundibugyo Ebola Outbreak

2026-05-27T16:00:00Z — cycle 18 (first run of May 27)

## What happened May 26

- **Uganda HCW cluster confirmed; alert issued.** Cases 6 and 7 are both healthcare workers at an unnamed private Kampala hospital, exposed through patients linked to Case 1. Uganda total: 7 confirmed / 1 death. Three of seven Uganda cases (43%) are HCWs — a nosocomial amplification signal in the capital. Uganda also suspended public transport to DRC.
- **Case count growing fast.** DRC hit 112 confirmed cases (Wikipedia, May 25-26), up from 101 on May 24 (+11%). Suspected cases held at ~906. By end of day, ECDC would update to 121 confirmed / 1,077 suspected.
- **$500M pledged at May 25 ministerial meeting.** Nearly doubled from $208M on May 23. Key donors: OCHA ($60M), US State Dept ($23M), Gates Foundation ($15M), South Africa ($5M). African nations covering ~10% domestically. Disbursement timelines unknown.
- **Obeldesivir named as PEP candidate.** WHO DG May 25 briefing: obeldesivir (Gilead GS-5245, oral remdesivir prodrug) being evaluated as post-exposure prophylaxis for high-risk contacts, alongside two monoclonal antibodies (MBP134 + Regeneron). Trial described as "being developed" with Africa CDC — not yet initiated as of May 26.
- **DON604 still absent.** WHO's next situation report is 5+ days overdue since DON603 (May 21). No new geographic spread confirmed.

## Why this matters

Uganda's HCW rate (43%) signals healthcare settings are becoming amplifying nodes, not just receiving patients — the same dynamic that accelerated the 2018-2020 DRC Zaire outbreak. If nosocomial chains develop in Kampala's private hospital sector, containment becomes significantly harder.
